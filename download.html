What the app does

  • 💬
    Real conversations. Ask anything, from quick facts to deep research, and it remembers the chat.
  • 🎨
    Makes and edits images. Describe a picture and get it in seconds. Open it full-screen, copy it or download it.
  • 👁️
    Understands your pictures. Paste a screenshot or upload a photo and ask about it.
  • 🎙️
    Voice in and out. Speak your question and have replies read back to you.
  • 🌐
    Live web search. Up-to-date answers with source cards.
  • 📄
    Files and code. Upload PDFs, Word, Excel, ZIPs or whole folders. Get code as files you can copy or download.
  • 🔔
    Works in the background. Switch apps while it thinks and get an IlmCoreAI notification when your answer is ready.

How to install

  1. When the download finishes, tap the notification or open Downloads and tap IlmCoreAI.apk.
  2. If Android asks, tap Settings and allow your browser to install unknown apps, then go back.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details → Install anyway. The app isn't from the Play Store.
  4. Open IlmCore AI and sign in or create a free account.

Android only. On iPhone, open IlmCore AI in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
