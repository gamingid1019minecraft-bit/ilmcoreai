Frequently asked questions

Everything you might want to know before signing up.

Is IlmCore AI really free?

Yes. There is no paid tier and no credit card required. All features — chat, image generation, voice, file upload — are available the moment you sign up.

What makes IlmCore different from other AI chatbots?

Three things: (1) it does many things at once — chat, images, voice, files, web search — in one place; (2) it's free without asterisks; (3) it uses multiple AI providers behind the scenes so it stays up when others go down.

Can IlmCore AI read my files?

Yes. Upload a PDF, DOCX, image, or an entire folder of code and ask questions about it. IlmCore extracts the text and reasoning from what you upload.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes — IlmCore AI runs entirely in your browser. It supports voice input (speak to it) and voice output (it reads replies to you) on both Android and iOS.

How do I switch to my own AI backend?

Open Settings → scroll to Advanced — custom backend → enter any OpenAI-compatible URL that serves /health, /auth/*, /sessions, and /chat → click Test then Save. The app reloads and points at your backend.
